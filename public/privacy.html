Privacy

Last updated 20 September 2026. Anbudan Amma is a small app, so this page is short and plain.

What our server keeps

  • Your reminder settings: language, mood, reminder times and quiet hours, plus your time zone.
  • The push address your browser gives us, so we can send notifications to this phone.
  • A private code that proves requests come from your phone. We keep only a scrambled (hashed) copy.
  • Small counters: how many reminders were sent today, how many deliveries failed, and how many chat messages you used today (removed after a few days).

There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

Chat with Amma

  • What you type is sent to our server, which passes it to an AI service to write Amma's reply. We do not save your messages. By default the AI service is Cloudflare Workers AI. The app's owner can switch it to Groq or Sarvam.
  • If a message sounds like you may be in serious distress, Amma replies with a fixed, caring message and does not send it to the AI.
  • The last 30 messages are kept on your phone only, until you tap "Clear chat".
  • Amma is an AI character, not a real person, and cannot give medical, legal or financial advice. Please do not share private details in chat.
  • If the app's owner has turned it on, you may see a button that speaks an AI reply aloud in a synthesized voice (currently Sarvam). That sentence is sent to Sarvam to generate the audio. This is separate from Amma's real recordings and from her pre-made voice clips, and is always shown as a different button so it is never mistaken for a real voice.

Your own Amma: photo and voice

  • A photo or recordings you add stay in your browser's storage on this phone. They are never uploaded to us.
  • The photo is also copied into your browser's cache so reminders can show it. Deleting them in the app, or clearing this site's data in your browser, removes them.
  • Please only record people who are happy to be recorded.

Voice and sound

  • The speaker button plays clips that come with the app, or your phone's built-in voice.
  • The microphone button in chat uses your browser's speech recognition. That service may send your voice to your browser's provider (for Chrome, Google). We never receive the audio.

Other services involved

  • Cloudflare hosts the app and stores the data above.
  • Notifications travel through your browser's push service (Google, Apple, Mozilla or Microsoft, depending on your browser). The message is encrypted between our server and your browser.
  • The app loads its fonts from Google Fonts.

Deleting your data

On the home screen, tap "Turn off Amma on this phone". That removes your schedule and push address from our server. Chat counters disappear within a few days. To remove what is stored on your phone, clear this site's data in your browser settings.
